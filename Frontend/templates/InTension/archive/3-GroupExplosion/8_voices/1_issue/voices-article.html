So,weredo you live?

Is it somewhere that is environmentally safe, politically stable, and free from conflict? There are many places where the answer is ‘no’ to all three conditions. But in this age of a deepening climate crisis, nowhere can be completely environmentally safe, either now or in the not-too-distant future. Politically, the number of unsafe places is increasing. More nations are becoming politically repressive. Likewise, nations that once were seemingly stable democracies have now rushed into, or drifted towards, authoritarian government. It’s become clear that political situations can, and do, change fast: no nation is immune from the disruption of its political foundations. At the same time, there is insecurity and a lack of faith in stability even in what appear to be stable political regimes. Governments are just not willing to deal with the existential dangers of the planet. They know this requires drastic action, which would make them unpopular, and so put their future at risk. As a reaction, they do nothing that has any real impact, thus they survive. Such inaction brings us back to the questions of safety and the issues of environmental, political, and conflict dangers. Inadequate action makes it certain that dangers will grow. In truth, nowhere is safe. There has to be another way. We are all between now and it, Safety will not be found,it has to be made, and this publication aims to be part of the making. Tell us about where you live in-tension from conflict, climate impacts and other critical conditions. A selection will be published on our website.

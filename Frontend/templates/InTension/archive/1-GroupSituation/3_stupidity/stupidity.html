Stupitidy

Stupidity is a structural element of the contemporary world. It is evidenced by: the continuation of practices that defuture; industries bringing products into being and marketing without any real understanding of what their consequences will be materially. Two immediate immaterial examples are the Internet and artificial intelligence. Likewise, war makes no sense, but a vast amount of economic and intellectual capital is invested in advancing the war machine—which adds up to the application of reason to serve unreason.

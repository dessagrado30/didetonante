Matters ofTension

The publication will address those concerns of life in the contemporary world — environmental, climatic, geopolitical, psycho-social, political, technological, cultural — that are reported, need to be identified, that demand our attention, are sources of tension, and that beg better understanding. Some of these concerns may touch our lives, confront us emotionally, intellectually or politically.

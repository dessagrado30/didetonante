Education-in-error

Education-in-error: the developmental form and content of the education system globally is structured toward tertiary education entrance. According to UNESCO this represents 40% of all people aged 18-24. The majority therefore can be argued to have been disadvantaged by this bias. Even more fundamental, education is underpinned by divisions of knowledge created during the European Enlightenment. These divisions directly link to professional practices, with education becoming dominantly instrumental and in the service of the economy. This modality of knowledge is at odds with the functional relational interconnectedness of Earth systems and ecologies. As a result, the way people learn to materially create, occupy and replicate the worlds of their existence has become unsustainable, negating the conditions that sustain life. To become futural, education needs to be able to prompt people to think and act relationally across all of their practices. While this is a huge challenge, it is not a choice. Unless a process of adaptive change is made over coming decades, our species and many others, will not survive. This imperative adds to the state of tension felt by many, as such it has to be a major issue of this publication’s agenda.

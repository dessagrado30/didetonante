ReEducation

Education is as much a part of the problem as it is a domain of potential solution. It fails in three ways: much of what is taught inducts students into unsustainable ways of thought and practice. By implication, much of what is taught comes from the past, reproduces the status quo, and is actually redundant. What (formal) education needs to do is to learn how to (i) respond to those conditions of the present and future that reproduce unsustainable knowledge, practices and values, and overcome them, and (ii) select knowledge from the past that has futural cultural value and agency, while advancing new knowledge and practices that advance sustainment.

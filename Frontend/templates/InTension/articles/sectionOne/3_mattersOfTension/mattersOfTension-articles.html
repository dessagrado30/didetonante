Living in 'the end times'.

The end times are the convergent terminal condition of a future negating now, and what comes after if these forces of negation are effectively resisted and the actuality of a condition of possibility is created. An Apocalyptic view of the future in which life ends has been a feature of many religions for as long as they have existed. Likewise, there is a real moment of ending which is known, but dismissed because it is so distant in time–the Sun is projected to die, and with it, life on Earth, this within five billion years. But talk of ‘the end times’ is saying something else. It speaks to a more immediate moment of finitude caused by our species' own actions. For example: a global nuclear war leading to a lifeless ‘nuclear winter,’ biological extinction due to an unchecked loss of biodiversity, escalating impacts of climate change, or a massive pandemic. Billions of people are aware of evidence indicating that such prospects are real, and their causal conditions are underway. Two questions then follow—can these events be prevented and how, and what comes after this action? What is the possible form of a new beginning, a new future?

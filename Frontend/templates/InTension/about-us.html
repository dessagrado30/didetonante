Meeting in the Borderland

The differences between us (Tony, Anne-Marie, Hector and Carolina) are many. Differences of time zones, languages, economy, work environment, age, education and culture. Yet we have created a space of knowledge, opinions, ideas, and concerns, and in doing so we work together in a place of exchange, a borderland. In-Tension is the product of our cooperative working arrangement built with time and care toward the other and their differences. Our aim through the publication, podcasts, projects and events is to expand this collaborative space.

The reason for creating In Tension can be seen in its content and how it inter-relates. Underpinning it is the notion of the ‘end times’ - which is not just a characterisation of the age, but also a mental, physical, and emotional state that impinges on so many of us in different areas, both within and outside the global North and South and their complexities. A key aspect of this is stupidity, which has become structural, as it can be seen in so many world systems and events. This situation prompts the need for a constructive and interwoven resistance that results particularly in changes in nature, content, and significance of education. Equally, in facing the facts of worlds of crisis, other futures have to be imagined beyond idealist fantasies and utopias. So viewed, we all find ourselves in a borderland between how things defuturingly are and how they need to futurally be. This situation can only change collectively.

Understanding the collective as a careful creation of boundaries and bridges, where we recognize that what we comprehend as the other, academically, geographically, and culturally is not distant or unrelated, but rather part of what defines our existence and becoming. This implies and will imply ruptures at various levels as well as disruptive conversations that challenge convictions of all kinds. As a team of in-tension, we are willing to engage in these conversations and extend an invitation to do so.

Thus, communication is essential for this collectivity, as a change community, to come into being. In-Tension is one modest contribution. It asserts a refusal of helplessness as life is lived in the end times. It is a product of difference, of a borderland, the invitation is open.

Our Ambition?

To create a space that gives voice to, and expresses the condition of tension that denotes the existential condition of being-in-the world now. To build a community of communication, a cross-readership dialogue, that also disseminates information, ideas, collaborative opportunities. To develop dialogue on critical issues in ways that prompt projects, action and events. To build an archive of ideas and images. To highlight politically underdeveloped and neglected issues and new ways of thinking about familiar ones. To enable a space of bilingual exchange between voices of the South and North in and beyond their respective geographies.
